    How to Play

    The goal: slide the red block out through the gap in the right-hand wall.

    Drag a block to slide it. Blocks only move along their length and can't pass through each other or walls.

    Tap to move (in Settings): tap a block, then tap the empty cell it should slide to.

    Par is the fewest moves the puzzle can be solved in. One slide counts as one move, however far it goes.

    Hint slides the best next block for you. Undo takes back a move. Puzzles where you use a hint don't count in your stats.
