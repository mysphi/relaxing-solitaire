    How to Play

    The goal: open every cell that isn't a mine. There's no clock, so take your time.

    Tap a cell to open it. Your first tap is always safe and opens up an area.

    Numbers tell you how many of the 8 cells around them hide a mine.

    Long-press a cell to flag a mine you've found. Long-press again for a question mark, then blank.

    Flag mode swaps the two, so a tap flags and a long-press opens. Handy for one hand.

    Tap a number that already has all its flags to open the rest of its neighbours in one go.

    Hint shows a cell that logic says is safe. Undo steps back, even after hitting a mine, but that game won't count in your stats.

    No-guessing boards (on by default) can always be solved by logic, so you never have to guess.
